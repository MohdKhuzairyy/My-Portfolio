Who is Khuzairy?

Hello! My name is Mohammad Khuzairy bin Binjamin, but most people call me Harry. I am from Sabah, Malaysia, and currently pursuing a Bachelor of Information Technology at Universiti Malaysia Sabah (UMS). I am someone who enjoys exploring technology, turning ideas into something real and learning something new along the way. Whether it is designing a website, developing a project or experimenting with creative digital tools, I always enjoy the process of figuring things out.

Outside my academic life, I enjoy sports, university activities and spending time with people who inspire me to become better. I also like taking on challenges that push me outside my comfort zone. I may be an Information Technology student, but I promise I do occasionally close my laptop and touch some grass. Occasionally.
