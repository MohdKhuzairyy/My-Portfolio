Project Overview

The BPPI UMS website was developed to provide a digital platform for presenting information about the department, its services, research activities and related resources.

The development focused on organising information into accessible sections and creating a consistent website layout that allows visitors to navigate the department's information more easily.

Website Pages and Features

Apart from the main homepage, the website includes other sections that provide visitors with useful departmental information and ways to get in touch.
